## OVERVIEW

The Master Document reveals **critical career intelligence**: physicians need to articulate their **career narrative** across 6 key domains for academic promotion:

1. **Teaching & Education** (direct teaching, curriculum, mentoring, evaluations)
2. **Research & Scholarship** (publications, grants, presentations, impact)
3. **Clinical Excellence** (scope, innovation, QI, patient outcomes)
4. **Service & Leadership** (committees, society roles, advocacy)
5. **Professional Identity** (coherent narrative arc, thematic focus)
6. **Future Vision** (goals, growth trajectory)

**FISCMAK's opportunity:** Coach Mak can guide physicians through **career narrative development** while simultaneously **building their promotion dossier**.

## ENHANCED ASSESSMENT DESIGN

### Touchpoint 1: Professional Identity Clarity
**Focus:** Career stage, specialty, promotion track, narrative theme

```
Q1.1: "What's your current academic rank?" 
      → Student, Resident, Fellow, Assistant Professor, Associate Professor, Full Professor

Q1.2: "What promotion track best describes your career focus?"
      → Clinician-Educator, Clinician-Scientist, Clinical Excellence, 
        Research Scientist, Clinician-Investigator, Administrative Leadership, Other

Q1.3: "In one sentence, how would you describe your professional identity?"
      → [open text] Maps to "narrative arc" domain

Q1.4: "What's your biggest career goal over the next 3-5 years?"
      → Promotion, External funding, Program leadership, Work-life balance, Pivot specialty, Other

Q1.5: "Rate your clarity on your career direction" 
      → Likert 1-5 (baseline for later comparison)

Q1.6: "Which of these academic domains feels like your primary focus?"
      → Teaching, Research, Clinical, Entrepreneurship, Administration, Multiple equally

Q1.7: "Do you have a mentor in your current track?"
      → Yes / No / Looking for one

Q1.8: "How well do you currently articulate your professional accomplishments?"
      → Likert 1-5 (identifies narrative development need)
```

### Touchpoint 2: Career Inventory (Post-CV Upload)
**Focus:** Mapped against promotion domains via CV extraction

**Mak performs structured extraction:**
```
FROM CV, MAK EXTRACTS & ASKS:

Teaching & Education:
- "I see you've taught [X] courses/clerkships. Tell me about one that had the most impact."
- "How many residents/fellows have you formally mentored?"
- "Any teaching awards or recognition?"
- "Have you developed curriculum innovations?"

Research & Scholarship:
- "What's been your primary research focus?"
- "How many first-author publications do you have?"
- "Have you secured external funding (grants)?"
- "What's your h-index or most-cited work?"

Clinical Excellence:
- "Describe your clinical role and scope"
- "Any quality improvement or patient safety innovations?"
- "Board certifications? Maintenance of certification status?"

Service & Leadership:
- "What institutional committees are you on?"
- "Any regional/national society leadership?"
- "Editorial board roles?"

Invisible Work:
- "Beyond your formal role, what non-billable activities consume significant time?"
- "Any mentoring outside formal structure?"
- "Committee work or advocacy?"
```

### Touchpoint 3: Wellbeing + Invisible Work Mapping
**Focus:** Burnout signals + recognition of undervalued contributions

```
Burnout Questions (Maslach-style):
Q3.1-3.4: Emotional exhaustion, depersonalization, efficacy, workload

Invisible Work Questions:
Q3.5: "How many mentees have you actively guided (formally or informally)?"
Q3.6: "Estimate hours/month on uncompensated service (committees, admin, mentoring)"
Q3.7: "Have you taken on diversity, equity, or inclusion work?"
Q3.8: "Rate how visible your contributions are to leadership"
      → Likert 1-5 ("My work is invisible to decision-makers" ↔ "My work is well-recognized")
```

**Mak Insight:** "I notice you're mentoring 8 residents informally, chairing 2 committees, and developing curriculum—that's significant leadership work that might not be evident in a CV scan. Let's make sure this gets counted."

## PROMOTION TRACK MAPPING

FISCMAK aligns 4 core pathways with **7 academic tracks**:

### Clinical Pathway Maps To:
- **Clinical Excellence Track** (high clinical volume, QI, patient outcomes)
- **Clinician-Educator Track** (if teaching + education scholarship)
- **Clinician-Investigator Track** (if some clinical research)

### Research Pathway Maps To:
- **Clinician-Scientist Track** (tenure, peer-reviewed research, extramural funding)
- **Clinician-Investigator Track** (translational/clinical research, may be non-tenure)
- **Research Scientist Track** (for PhD scientists)

### Education Pathway Maps To:
- **Clinician-Educator Track** (teaching + educational scholarship + mentoring)
- **Educator Track** (dedicated full-time educators, some institutions)

### Leadership Pathway Maps To:
- **Administrative Leadership Track** (program/department leadership)
- **Health Services/Population Health Track** (if epidemiology/policy focus)
- **Innovation/Entrepreneurship Track** (if patents, startups, commercialization)

**Mak identifies track based on:**
1. User's stated focus (TP 1: "Clinician-Educator vs Clinician-Scientist?")
2. CV analysis (TP 2: actual time allocation across domains)
3. Values clarification (TP 4: what user prioritizes)
4. Market data (TP 6: what's valued in market)

## COACH MAK'S PROMOTION-SPECIFIC BEHAVIORS

### Behavior 1: Narrative Mirror
**When:** User describes career progress
**Action:** "I'm hearing that you're an expert in [clinical domain], you've mentored [X] residents, you've published [Y] papers, and you've led [Z] quality initiatives. Your coherent narrative might be: '[Name] is a [Clinical Excellence / Clinician-Educator / Clinician-Scientist] focused on [theme]. Here's where that takes you for promotion...'"

### Behavior 2: Invisible Work Surfacing
**When:** CV is uploaded
**Action:** OCR extracts CV, but Mak asks follow-ups:
- "I see [X formal roles]. What about mentoring, committees, or advocacy work? Those count for promotion but often aren't in CVs."
- "You mentioned you chair [committee]—how much time does that consume? That's institutional impact that belongs in your narrative."

### Behavior 3: Domain-Specific Coaching
**When:** User identifies gap (e.g., "I have no publications")
**Action:** "Let's work on that. Are you on a Research/Scholarship track? What would success look like? Would you aim for peer-reviewed education publications, grant submissions, or a different type of scholarship?"

### Behavior 4: Promotion Timeline Reality Check
**When:** User sets ambitious goals
**Action:** "Promoting from Assistant → Associate typically requires [X years], [Y publications], [Z funding]. You're at [current state]. Here's the gap. Here's a realistic 3-year plan..."

### Behavior 5: External Validation
**When:** User questions market value
**Action:** "Based on current job market, your profile (Cardiologist with 8 years, [research/teaching], [leadership]) commands [salary range] in [top markets]. You're [below/at/above] market. Let's talk about options..."

### Behavior 6: Dossier Builder
**When:** User is close to promotion
**Action:** "Based on our conversations, here's a draft narrative arc. [Pulls promotion narrative template from Master Document]. Does this feel right? Should we emphasize [Domain A] more or [Domain B]?"

## EXAMPLE: CLINICIAN-EDUCATOR PROMOTION JOURNEY IN FISCMAK

**Month 0 (Tier 1: Onboarding)**
- User selects "Clinician-Educator" track in TP1
- Mak: "Great! For Associate Professor promotion, you'll need excellence in teaching, educational scholarship, mentorship, and clinical care. Let's build that case."

**Month 1 (Tier 2: CV Upload)**
- User uploads CV
- Mak extraction identifies: 10 hrs/year teaching, no publications, mentored 2 residents formally
- Mak: "I see strong clinical foundations. For promotion, let's focus on educational scholarship and visibility of your mentorship. Have you published anything from your teaching innovations?"

**Month 3 (TP 3: Wellbeing + Invisible Work)**
- Assessment reveals: 15 hours/month on uncompensated mentoring, curriculum committee
- Mak: "You're investing heavily in invisible work. That's leadership. Let's make sure it counts. Would you like to formalize one of these mentoring relationships or publish your curriculum work?"

**Month 6 (TP 4: Values + Narrative)**
- User clarifies: "I'm passionate about developing next-generation educators through simulation."
- Mak: "Perfect. That's your narrative arc: 'Clinical educator developing clinical educators through simulation-based education.' Now, what peer-reviewed work can demonstrate that?"

**Month 9 (TP 5: Skill Gaps)**
- Assessment reveals: Teaching ✅ Strong | Scholarship ⚠️ Weak | Clinical ✅ Strong | Service ✓ Active
- Mak: "Your gap is clear: educational scholarship. Let's plan a publication. You could submit a curriculum paper to MedEd Portal or submit a study proposal to your education research network."

**Month 12 (TP 6: Market Validation)**
- Job market data shows: Clinician-educators with simulation expertise in high demand at major academic centers
- Mak: "Your profile is increasingly valuable. You have negotiating power. But to accelerate promotion, let's target 1-2 publications in next 12 months. Here's a realistic timeline..."

**Month 15 (Promotion Readiness Check)**
- Mak generates **promotion readiness profile:**
  - Teaching: 95/100 (ready)
  - Scholarship: 40/100 (needs work—1 publication in progress)
  - Clinical: 90/100 (ready)
  - Service: 70/100 (ready)
  - Narrative: 85/100 (coherent)
  - **Overall: 70/100 (Ready in 12-18 months, not now)**

- Recommended action plan:
  1. Target publication by month 18
  2. Expand teaching hours to 15/year
  3. Pursue regional simulation education society role
  4. Draft promotion narrative by month 18
  5. Submit dossier by month 24

**Month 18-24 (TP 7: Accountability + Narrative Drafting)**
- Mak generates draft promotion narrative using Master Document template:
  - Professional Identity: [Coherent simulation educator narrative]
  - Teaching: [Teaching hours, awards, courses developed, evaluations]
  - Scholarship: [1-2 publications, grant applications, presentations]
  - Clinical: [Active practice, board certified, patient outcomes]
  - Service: [Committee + regional society role]
  - Future: [Vision for next 3-5 years]

- User reviews, edits, and submits to promotion committee
- **Promotion granted within 24 months** ✅
